# Credits and sources

The map is the work of Brendan Dawe. These are the sources it is drawn from. Where a licence asks for attribution, it is given here and on
the page.

## Timetables

- Timetable scans from **Timetableworld.com**: the *Official Guide of the Railways* (April/May 1948), Canadian Pacific (1948), Canadian
  National (1949), Great Northern, Spokane International, Esquimalt & Nanaimo and Pacific Great Eastern timetables. The scans are not
  reproduced here; the transcriptions in `pipeline/` are of the times only. (Sources of the other scans still to be recorded.)
- Union Steamship Co. of B.C. Sailing Guide No. 138 (31 May 1948), via ferriesbc.proboards.com.
- Gulf Islands Ferry Co. 1941 summer schedule (Brendan Dawe's copy).
- Some services are reconstructed from described frequencies or estimated; each says so in its notes on the map.

## Track

- **National Railway Network (NRWN)**, Natural Resources Canada, under the Open Government Licence – Canada.
- **North American Rail Network / NTAD**, U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics (White Pass in Alaska, Alaska Railroad).
- **Canadian Electric Railway Mapping Project** and the **Ontario Railway Map Collection**, Paul Delamere (B.C. Electric routes, the Lakehead).
- Railway KMLs by **Tony Howe and David Price, Mississippi Rails** (Washington, Oregon and the Midwest: Great Northern, Northern
  Pacific, Soo Line, Chicago & North Western and others).
- Canadian Northern and Grand Trunk Pacific track sets from the maps in the Wikipedia articles
  ["Canadian Northern Railway"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Northern_Railway) and
  ["Grand Trunk Pacific Railway"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Trunk_Pacific_Railway), CC BY-SA.
- Abandoned and disused lines in Saskatchewan and Manitoba from **OpenStreetMap**, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- Kettle Valley line: [kettlevalleyrailway.ca](https://www.kettlevalleyrailway.ca/map.html).
- Brendan Dawe's own traces (Google Earth and OpenRailwayMap; OpenRailwayMap data © OpenStreetMap contributors).

## Places and water

- **Canadian Geographical Names Database**, Natural Resources Canada (Open Government Licence – Canada).
- Washington place names: Washington State Department of Ecology's copy of the U.S. Geological Survey GNIS.
- Lakes, rivers and coast: **Statistics Canada** (Open Government Licence – Canada) and the Washington State Department of Ecology.
- Province, state and international borders: **Natural Earth** 1:10m (public domain).
