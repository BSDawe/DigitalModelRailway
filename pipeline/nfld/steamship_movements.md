# Steamship movements, The Daily News, St. John's, July 1948 (transcribed by Brendan, 13 Nov 2026)

## July 2
Furness Red Cross Line
- S.S. Fort Amherst: sailed June 30 at 11 a.m. for Halifax and New York.
- S.S. Fort Townshend left New York June 30th. Leaving Halifax July 3rd. Due St. John's July 5. Sailing July 7 for Halifax and New York.

Blue Peter
- M.V. Atlantic Charter en route Halifax, leaves Halifax July 3, due St. John's July 7. Leaves St. John's July 9th. Leaves Halifax July 15th. Leaves
  North Sydney July 16th. Due St. John's July 19th.

Clarke Steamship Co.
- S.S. Island Connector leaving Montreal July 3rd. Leaving Charlottetown July 8th for St. John's. Due St. John's July 20th [sic].
- S.S. Gulfport loading at Montreal July 5 - 7th. Sailing July 12th via Corner Brook. Due at St. John's July 20th.

## July 3
Furness Red Cross
- Fort Townshend leaves Halifax July 3. Due St. John's July 5, sailing July 7th for Halifax and New York.
- Fort Amherst: at Halifax en route to New York. Due New York Monday, July 5. Enroute Bermuda. Leaving New York again July 17th for Halifax and
  St. John's. Due St. John's July 22. Sailing July 24th.

Railway Boats
- Kyle sails 4 p.m. Saturday on Labrador Service.
- Burgeo fogbound at St. Lawrence outward.
- Bar Haven still fogbound at Fortune inward.
- Springdale left Change Islands noon inward.
- Glencoe left Moreton's Hr. 10.45 a.m. inward to St. John's.
- Northern Ranger last report left Battle Hr. 6 a.m. Wednesday outward to Corner Brook.
- Codroy at Corner Brook.

Blue Peter
- M.V. Atlantic Charter en route Halifax. Leaves Halifax July 3, due St. John's July 9th [7th on July 2 and 5]. Leaves Halifax July 15th. Leaves
  North Sydney July 16th. Due St. John's July 19th.

Clarke Steamship Co.
- S.S. Island Connector leaving Montreal July 3. Leaving Charlottetown July 8th for St. John's. Due St. John's July 11th. Sailing July 12th for Montreal.
- S.S. Gulfport loading at Montreal July 5 - 7th. Sailing July 12th via Corner Brook. Due St. John's July 20th.

## July 5
Blue Peter
- Atlantic Charter en route to St. John's, due July 7th. Leaves St. John's July 9th. Leaves Halifax July 15th. Leaves North Sydney July 16th, due at
  St. John's July 19th. Leaves St. John's July 20th.

Furness Red Cross
- Fort Townshend leaving Halifax July 3. Due St. John's July 5th. Sailing July 7th for Halifax and New York.
- Fort Amherst at Halifax en route to New York. Due New York Monday July 5th. Leaving New York again July 17th for Halifax and St. John's. Due St.
  John's July 22nd. Sailing July 24th.

Clarke
- Island Connector leaving Charlottetown July 9th for St. John's. Due St. John's July 11th. Sailing July 12th for Montreal.
- Gulfport loading at Montreal July 5th - 7th. Sailing July 12th via Corner Brook. Due at St. John's July 20th.

## July 6
Blue Peter
- Atlantic Charter: [as before; due St. John's July 7, leaves St. John's July 9]. Leaves Halifax July 13. Leaves St. John's July 20th. Leaves Halifax
  July 26th. Leaves North Sydney July 27th. Due St. John's July 30th.

Furness Red Cross
- Fort Townshend: arrived in port 9 a.m. [Monday July 5]. Sailing Wednesday July 7th for Halifax and New York.
- Fort Amherst: en route to Bermuda. Leaving for Halifax and St. John's. Due St. John's July 22nd, sailing July 24th.

Clarke (advertised for passengers, both ships: Brendan)
- Island Connector leaving Charlottetown July 8th for St. John's. Due St. John's July 11th. Sailing July 12th for Montreal.

Railway Boats
- Kyle left Englee 6.15 a.m. yesterday [Monday July 5], outward.
- Burgeo left Jersey Harbour 9.20 a.m. yesterday [July 5], outward.
- Bar Haven: this morning's train to Argentia will make connection for Bay Route, Placentia Bay [as in the railway notice of Monday July 5].
- Springdale left Springdale 9 a.m. outward.
- Glencoe leaving St. John's 10 a.m. Thursday for regular ports to Lewisporte.
- Northern Ranger: today's express [Tuesday July 6, the foreign express] will make connection at Corner Brook for regular ports to St. John's.
- Codroy: Wednesday's express connection at Corner Brook for regular ports to Battle Harbour.

## July 7
Blue Peter
- Atlantic Charter en route to St. John's, due July 7th. Leaves St. John's July 9th. Leaves Halifax July 13th. Leaves North Sydney July 16th. Due St. John's
  July 19th. Leaves St. John's July 20th. Leaves Halifax July 26th. Leaves North Sydney July 27th. Due St. John's July 30th.

Furness Red Cross
- Fort Townshend in port. Sailing Wednesday July 7th for Halifax and New York.
- Fort Amherst: en route to Bermuda. Leaving New York again July 17th for Halifax and St. John's. Due St. John's July 22nd. Sailing July 24th.

Clarke
- Island Connector leaving Charlottetown July 8th for St. John's. Due St. John's July 11th. Sailing July 12th for Montreal.

Furness Warren (mentioned mostly for June dates before this)
- S.S. [R.M.S.] Newfoundland due at St. John's July 12th from Boston and Halifax, sailing same day for Liverpool.
- Earlier notices: left Liverpool June 23rd; sailing from St. John's for Halifax and Boston June 30th.

Railway Boats
- Kyle stormbound at St. Andrew['s].
- Burgeo left Cape La Hune 2.45 p.m. yesterday [July 6], outward.
- Bar Haven left Sound Island 11.50 a.m. yesterday [July 6], outward.
- Springdale left Little Bay 3.30 p.m. yesterday [July 6], inward.
- Glencoe sailing from St. John's 10 a.m. Thursday for regular ports to Lewisporte.
- Northern Ranger expected to leave Corner Brook some time this evening [July 7] for regular ports to St. John's.
- Codroy leaving Corner Brook to-day [July 7], special trip to North Sydney.

## July 8
Blue Peter
- Atlantic Charter: en route to St. John's, due July 7 [sic, as July 7]. Leaves July 9th. Leaves Halifax July 13th. Leaves North Sydney July 16th. Due
  St. John's July 19th. Leaves St. John's July 20th. Leaves Halifax July 26th. Leaves North Sydney July 27th. Due St. John's July 30th.

Furness Red Cross
- Fort Townshend: sailing today [Thursday July 8] for Halifax and New York. [July 6 and 7 said Wednesday July 7: she sailed a day late.]
- Fort Amherst: en route to Bermuda. Leaving New York again July 17th for Halifax and St. John's. Due St. John's July 22nd. Sailing July 24th.

Clarke
- Island Connector leaving Charlottetown July 8th for St. John's. Due St. John's July 11th. Sailing July 12th for Montreal.

Furness Warren
- S.S. Newfoundland due St. John's July 12 from Boston and Halifax, sailing same day for Liverpool. (Same details for the other direction as before.)

Railway Boats [word for word as July 7 except the Kyle: probably the July 7 report reprinted, so "yesterday" = July 6]
- Kyle stormbound at St. Anthony, outward. [July 7 transcription: St. Andrew; St. Anthony fits the Labrador run.]
- Burgeo left Cape La Hune 2.45 p.m. yesterday, outward.
- Bar Haven left Sound Island 11.50 a.m. yesterday, outward.
- Springdale left Little Bay 3.30 p.m. yesterday, inward.
- Glencoe sailing from St. John's 10 a.m. Thursday for regular ports to Lewisporte.
- Northern Ranger expected to leave Corner Brook some time this evening for regular ports to St. John's.
- Codroy leaving Corner Brook today, special trip to North Sydney.
