# Track ownership rules (drawing style = owner; trains keep their own colours)

- New Westminster (Fraser River) bridge, 1904: federal structure -> drawn as CN.
  BCER Chilliwack trains and GN trains cross it on CN-styled track.
- New Westminster -> Vancouver: GN's line (incl. Grandview Cut) -> drawn as GN.
  CN and GN trains share it; they split only for their own Vancouver terminals
  (CN station on Main St / GN station just north of it).
- Kettle Valley, Brookmere -> Hope -> Odlum: CP.
- CP main line, Mission-Huntingdon branch, Coquitlam-New Westminster branch: CP.
- BCER lines: BCER interurban style as now.

# Colour rules (from Brendan)
- Now: BCER cars keep per-line colours.
- Once mainline railways are added: all BCER traffic bright red (historic interurban colour, token --bcer);
  CP traffic railway burgundy (token --cp). Line identity then carried by station rings / panel, not car colour.
