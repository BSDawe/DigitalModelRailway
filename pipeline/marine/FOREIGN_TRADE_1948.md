# Foreign Trade, 26 June 1948: ocean sailing schedules (extracts for the map)

Source: *Foreign Trade* (weekly), Foreign Trade Service, Department of Trade and Commerce, Ottawa, 26 June 1948, "Transportation: Ocean-Going
Sailing Schedules", pp. 1293 - 1302 (PDF pp. 45 - 54; Brendan, 15 Nov 2026). Scan in the private repo: pipeline/scans/marine/foreign_trade_1948/.
These are CARGO LOADING DATES (a ship sails at or after the end of the range), furnished by the companies; subject to change. Only ships that
bear on the map (passenger ships, or the Newfoundland and Bermuda services) are copied here. (r) = refrigerated space.

## What it adds (Claude, 15 Nov 2026)
- Fort Townshend loads at Halifax July 6 - 9 for Bermuda (and Fort Amherst July 23 - 26): the Furness ships' Bermuda leg runs from Halifax,
  by way of New York, which agrees with the Daily News of July 9 (Fort Townshend: "en route New York via Halifax, due New York July 12,
  en route Bermuda").
- R.M.S. Newfoundland loads at Halifax July 3 - 6 for St. John's and Liverpool: the Halifax call of her northbound trip (due St. John's July 12
  from Boston and Halifax, Daily News July 7).
- C.N. "Lady boats" (Lady Nelson, Lady Rodney: Canadian National Steamships' passenger service to Bermuda, the Eastern Caribbean and British Guiana)
  sail from Halifax, Lady Nelson after July 8 - 16, Lady Rodney after July 16 - 27; * = from Saint John about three days earlier.
- Transatlantic passenger liners in the St. Lawrence and off Halifax: Ascania, Empress of Canada, Aquitania, Beaverbrae (and Donaldson's Laurentia).
