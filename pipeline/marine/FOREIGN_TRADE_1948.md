# Foreign Trade, 26 June 1948: ocean sailing schedules (extracts for the map)

Source: *Foreign Trade* (weekly), Foreign Trade Service, Department of Trade and Commerce, Ottawa, 26 June 1948, "Transportation: Ocean-Going
Sailing Schedules", pp. 1293 - 1302 (PDF pp. 45 - 54; Brendan, 15 Nov 2026). Scan in the private repo: pipeline/scans/marine/foreign_trade_1948/.
These are CARGO LOADING DATES (a ship sails at or after the end of the range), furnished by the companies; subject to change. Only ships that
bear on the map (passenger ships, or the Newfoundland and Bermuda services) are copied here. (r) = refrigerated space.

## What it adds (Claude, 15 Nov 2026)
- Fort Townshend loads at Halifax July 6 - 9 for Bermuda (and Fort Amherst July 23 - 26): the Furness ships' Bermuda leg runs from Halifax,
  by way of New York, which agrees with the Daily News of July 9 (Fort Townshend: "en route New York via Halifax, due New York July 12,
  en route Bermuda").
- R.M.S. Newfoundland loads at Halifax July 3 - 6 for St. John's and Liverpool: the Halifax call of her northbound trip (due St. John's July 12
  from Boston and Halifax, Daily News July 7).
- C.N. "Lady boats" (Lady Nelson, Lady Rodney: Canadian National Steamships' passenger service to Bermuda, the Eastern Caribbean and British Guiana)
  sail from Halifax, Lady Nelson after July 8 - 16, Lady Rodney after July 16 - 27; * = from Saint John about three days earlier.
- Transatlantic passenger liners in the St. Lawrence and off Halifax: Ascania, Empress of Canada, Aquitania, Beaverbrae (and Donaldson's Laurentia).

# Ocean liners for the week of 5 July 1948 (Brendan, 15 Nov 2026: transatlantic and transpacific passenger ships, drawn to the edge of the map;
# arrivals in Canada estimated where they fall in the week)
- CP, Montreal - Quebec - Liverpool, summer season 1948 (CP public timetable; scan in the private repo, scans/marine/cp_1948_atlantic_summer.png):
  Empress of Canada leaves Montreal 11.00 a.m. EST Saturday July 3, arrives Liverpool July 10 (in the Gulf and the Strait of Belle Isle on
  Monday 5); next leaves Liverpool July 14, Quebec July 20, Montreal July 21 (after the week). Empress of France from Sept. 22.
  Passenger-carrying freighters Beaverburn and Beaverford, 12 passengers, about twice monthly to Liverpool, eight days, dates on request
  (Foreign Trade: Beaverford loads at Montreal July 11 - 18, Beaverburn July 27).
- Pier 21 ship arrival database (https://pier21.ca/immigration-records/ship-arrival-database): arrivals by ship, day, month, ports, company;
  filterable by ship name in a browser (not by date). Empress of Canada 1948: June 10, June 30, July 21, Aug. 11.
  Its own note (Brendan, 15 Nov 2026): passenger ships calling at Pier 21, Halifax, 1928 - 1971; dates are SCHEDULED arrivals (occasionally
  a day or two off); incomplete (1960 and 1962 - 1966 missing; some refugee-organisation charters not listed). The Empress of Canada rows
  match CP's Montreal arrival dates (June 30, July 21), so a ship's date there may be its main Canadian port, not Halifax. Ships that never
  called at Halifax (Donaldson's Glasgow - Montreal ships, the Beaver freighters) are likely absent.
- Cunard Line - Donaldson Atlantic Line brochure, "The Historic St. Lawrence River Route to Europe", 1948 (GG Archives,
  https://www.ggarchives.com/OT/Brochures/CunardDAL-1948-HistoricStLawrenceSeawayToEurope.html; Brendan, 15 Nov 2026): "Canadian Service
  Oil-Burning Liners" from Montreal and Quebec: Cunard Franconia, Scythia, Samaria (20,000 tons), Ascania (14,500) to Liverpool, London,
  Southampton, Havre (first and tourist class); Donaldson Laurentia (8,350) and Lismoria (8,000) to Glasgow (first class only). "Frequent
  sailings"; no dates in the brochure. The Belle Isle route open to liners from the end of May to early November.

# Canadian National Steamships, "Bermuda and the West Indies" folder, 1948 (printer's mark "48"; https://streamlinermemories.info/CAN/CN48PortsofCall.pdf;
# Brendan, 17 Nov 2026; read by Claude through a text extract)
- Lady liners (Lady Rodney, Lady Nelson): southbound from Halifax; northbound end at Saint John. Southbound ports in order: Bermuda, St. Kitts, Antigua,
  Montserrat, Dominica, St. Lucia, Barbados, St. Vincent, Grenada, Trinidad, British Guiana (Georgetown). No dates or frequency printed; Boston not named.
  This fits Foreign Trade's "sails from Saint John about three days earlier" against the Halifax loading dates (Lady Nelson July 8 - 16, Lady Rodney July 16 - 27).
- Motor vessels Canadian Challenger, Canadian Cruiser, Canadian Constructor: 12 passengers (five double and two single rooms); no fixed schedule, four to six
  week voyages; in the open season southbound from and northbound ending at Montreal, in winter Halifax / Saint John. Foreign Trade: Canadian Cruiser loads at
  Montreal July 7 - 15, Canadian Challenger July 21 - 29, Canadian Constructor Aug. 10 - 17 (for Bermuda, the Leewards, Windwards and British Guiana).
- C.N. cargo steamers to the Bahamas and Jamaica: freight only, no dates.
