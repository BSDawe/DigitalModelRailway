S.S. NORONIC, Northern Navigation Co. (a Canada Steamship Lines subsidiary), Windsor - Detroit - Sarnia - Sault Ste. Marie - Port Arthur - Duluth
(Brendan, 3 Nov 2026; timetable page from the May 1948 Official Guide, supplied as a screenshot, not kept in the repo; points in traces/lakes/northern_navigation_co.kmz).

THE PAGE. Headed "Midsummer": westbound (read down) "June 25 to September 3", eastbound (read up) "June 29 to September 7". "All times shown are Eastern
Standard time, except where Central time is indicated thus (C.T.)": only Duluth. The year is not printed. June 25 and September 3 are Fridays and June 29 and
September 7 are Tuesdays, which is the 1948 calendar (also 1937 and 1943). Taken as summer 1948 (Brendan: "the SS Noronic"; she ran to 1949).

One round trip a week: the ship is at Windsor from 4.45 p.m. to 9.30 p.m. on Fridays.

WHAT IS PRINTED AND WHAT IS NOT. Every time above is printed except two: Detroit has only the westbound departure (11.00 p.m.) and the eastbound arrival
(3.30 p.m.). Estimated (shown "est."): westbound arrival at Detroit 9.50 p.m. (20 min from Windsor, 0.7 mi, a stop of 70 min); eastbound departure 4.30 p.m.
(a stop of 60 min, then 15 min to Windsor). The Detroit stop is therefore shown "est." on both sailings.

THE CLOCK. The map has one Pacific clock. Eastern times are shown 3 hours earlier. Duluth (Central) is printed an hour behind Eastern: 7.30 a.m. C.T. westbound
arrival = 8.30 a.m. Eastern = 5.30 a.m. on the map; 4.30 p.m. C.T. departure = 5.30 p.m. Eastern = 2.30 p.m. on the map. Westbound sailing starts Friday 6.30 p.m. map
time and arrives Tuesday 5.30 a.m. (3 days 11 hours, carried over into the next week as the page does for every long sailing). Eastbound starts Tuesday 2.30 p.m.,
ends Friday 1.45 p.m.

LIVERY. "csl": Canada Steamship Lines red (Brendan: "the same livery, perhaps a bright red if that isn't taken"). B.C. Electric uses a bright red (#d7261e) for every interurban,
so the CSL colour is a warmer vermilion (#d93a1c light, #ff7048 dark), Claude's pick, not confirmed. The funnel takes the same red. Any other Canada Steamship Lines
service (Georgian Bay, the Toronto - Montreal boats, the Saguenay) should use livery csl.
