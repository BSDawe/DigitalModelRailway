Barkley Sound Transportation Co., Port Alberni, summer 1948: ESTIMATED / MODEL schedule (Brendan, 13 Oct 2026; details scarce). Day round
trips from Port Alberni, leaving 8.00 a.m., six days a week, the two boats on alternate days; no Sunday sailing.
  Mon, Wed, Fri  M.V. Uchuck I:  Port Alberni - Franklin River - Kildonan - Ecoole - Sarita - Bamfield, and back the same way.
  Tue, Thu, Sat  M.V. Uchuck II: Port Alberni - Sechart - Broken Group Islands - Ucluelet, and back the same way.
Order of the Bamfield calls: Claude's suggestion (Brendan listed Franklin River, Sarita River, Kildonan, Ecoole, Bamfield): Kildonan and
Ecoole lie north of Sarita, and the Princess Maquinna's printed order (Bamfield, Sarita Bay, Ecoole, Kildonan, Green Cove, Franklin River)
is exactly this route reversed.
Rough distances (straight lines x1.25): Bamfield run about 48 nm each way, Ucluelet run about 45-50 nm. At about 10 knots with short calls:
Bamfield about 1.00 p.m., back at Port Alberni about 6.30 p.m.; Ucluelet about 1.30 p.m., back about 7.00 p.m. Calls timed by distance.
Points: Port Alberni (map / names file), Franklin River (Brendan, est), Kildonan, Ecoole, Sarita (Landing), Bamfield, Ucluelet (names file);
Sechart and the Broken Group call point needed (names file has Broken Group Islands 48.9000,-125.3389 only as the group).
Uchuck II: bought for the 1948 season (Brendan, confirmed).

(Brendan, 13 Oct 20:06) The boats carried the mail. Sunday: an excursion leaving Port Alberni 10.00 a.m. for Bamfield.
Working model (Claude): mail runs are same-day round trips as above (out 8.00 a.m., back the same evening). Sunday excursion by the
Uchuck I (the Bamfield boat): Port Alberni 10.00 a.m. direct to Bamfield (about 1.30 p.m.), about 1 1/2 hours there, back at Port Alberni
about 6.30 p.m. (no intermediate calls unless Brendan says otherwise). The two boats then make sense as two mail contracts (Bamfield side
and Ucluelet side) each with its own boat and days, plus the Sunday excursion.

SOURCES (Brendan, 13 Oct 20:11): British Colonist, 18 August 1949 (the 8.00 a.m. regular schedule; the Sunday excursion). Jan Peterson,
"Here Today, Here Tomorrow: A History of Port Alberni", talk to the Nanaimo Historical Society, 8 February 2001 (transcribed by Dalys Barney,
Vancouver Island University Library, 24 June 2020): the Uchuck ("quiet waters") began in 1946 as a mail and passenger service; three times a
week she left Port Alberni at 8 a.m., calling at Franklin River, Kildonan, Green Cove and Bamfield, then made the two-hour run across the mouth
of Barkley Sound to Ucluelet and Port Albion, and returned to Port Alberni at 8 o'clock at night.
PROPOSED (Claude, for Brendan's OK): one loop route, run by both boats on alternate days (Uchuck I Mon/Wed/Fri, Uchuck II Tue/Thu/Sat), so the
mail goes six days a week: Port Alberni 8.00 a.m. - Franklin River - Kildonan - Green Cove - Bamfield (about 12.30 p.m.) - across the mouth of
the Sound (2 hours) - Ucluelet (about 2.30 p.m.) - Port Albion - back by Sechart and the Broken Group (Brendan's earlier list) - Port Alberni
8.00 p.m. About 100 nm in 12 hours: about 9 knots with the calls. Sunday excursion as above (Uchuck I, 10.00 a.m., Bamfield).
Sunday excursion (Brendan, 20:13): no mail, likely no intermediate stops: direct both ways. Port Alberni 10.00 a.m. - Bamfield about 1.15 p.m.
(about 33 nm at 10 knots), 2 hours ashore, leave 3.15 p.m., Port Alberni about 6.30 p.m.
Points (Brendan, 20:15): Sechart (Sechart Lodge) 48.956582,-125.256047; Broken Group (est) 48.910303,-125.291491: no regular stop, a pier
where the boat would meet fishermen trading; mark as estimated. The loop is agreed; all points now in hand.
