# CN Montreal District employee timetable No. 16 (20 June 1948) compared with the model

Source: CN Central Region, Montreal District Time Table No. 16, effective 12.01 a.m. Sunday 20 June 1948 (Brendan, 12 Nov 2026; scan, 39 pages).
Compared with the model's CN files, which come from the Official Guide of 25 April 1948. Passenger and mixed trains only (freight left out).
Lines in the United States (Lewiston, Norway and Berlin subdivisions; Island Pond - Norton; Massena - Helena) were not compared.

How to read the employee timetable: a time with **S** is a regular stop, **F** a flag stop, **L** leave, **A** arrive; a time with no letter is
the time the train passes (no stop). Stations below count as passenger stops only where some passenger or mixed train has S, F, L or A there.

The employee timetable is the summer timetable (20 June); the model is April. Train differences may be seasonal, not omissions.

## Days that differ (probably the summer timetable)

- 30 (Montreal - St. Hyacinthe): daily (model Mon - Fri, with 230 on Saturday).
- 280 (Cornwall sub. suburban, eastward): daily (model Sunday).
- 621 (Valleyfield - Coteau): daily (model ex Sun); 622 daily (model ex Sun); the model's 623 / 624 (Sunday) are not in the employee timetable.
- 100 (Montfort sub.): reads "Sun. only" (model daily except Monday); small print, to check.

## Matches

Sherbrooke, St. Hyacinthe, St. Judes, Hemmingford, Granby, Lemoyne, Rouses Point, Massena, Cornwall, Alexandria, Beachburg, Renfrew,
Vankleek, Oka, Montfort, L'Assomption and Rawdon trains otherwise match the model's trains and days. The Oka subdivision numbers its
trains 381 - 399 / 382 - 398 between Oka Jct. and Calumet Beach; the model runs the Montfort subdivision's numbers through to Calumet Beach
(the same 13 northward and 12 southward trains).
