# Official Guide, May 1948: Canadian National pages (Brendan, 4 October 2026)

Filed for the Alberta build, which waits for Brendan's word. Not yet transcribed. See STATE.md for the project.

Pages: Table 75 (Winnipeg-Saskatoon-Edmonton main line), 166 (Edmonton-Jasper-Prince Rupert), 168 (Winnipeg-Kindersley-Drumheller-Calgary),
171 (Winnipeg-Regina-Saskatoon-Edmonton by Lloydminster and Vegreville), 179a-c (Northern Alberta: Hines Creek, Barrhead, Waterways, as
reprinted in the CN section), 193 (Drumheller-Trefoil), 203-219 and 210-215 (Alberta branches), 230 (Unity-Bodo), the Edmonton-Jasper-Vancouver
table already used, and the key to the signs.

Brendan's answers for the Alberta build (4 October):
- Scope: Alberta, to the Saskatchewan boundary.
- One Pacific clock: Alberta's Mountain times are shown an hour earlier, as now.
- Trains that carry on into Saskatchewan end at the last timed station in Alberta (no estimated time at the boundary).
- Track in neither the national network nor the backup sets: draw a straight estimate, flag it, and tell him. He has a quick trace of the
  Barrhead branch: sent 4 October, filed as traces/nar_barrhead_branch.tsv (two lines of lon/lat in WKT: Barrhead to the main line at
  Busby, 26.3 mi against 26.2 printed, meeting the network within 3 m; and a 0.2 mi second leg of the wye at the junction).
- Page heading: no preference, so it stays "Railways of British Columbia".
- National network routings first; the Canadian Northern and Grand Trunk Pacific sets are backups (backup_track.py).

Dashed lines (Brendan, 4 October 2026, not built yet): only track filled in with a straight line between stations is drawn dashed.
NTAD track stays a normal line (the White Pass is left alone), and so do his own Google Earth and OpenRailwayMap traces. So today nothing on
the map changes; it applies to the S.I. estimate into Spokane Union Station once that is done, and to every straight estimate in the Alberta build.

River boats (Brendan, 4 October 2026): the Hudson's Bay Company boats ran until 1948 (his reading); a schedule may exist but he does not think
it is in the Official Guide. Table 179a-c (Hines Creek, Barrhead, Waterways) carries no boat connection note. Left out unless a schedule turns up.
