# Canadian Pacific timetable, 1948: Alberta pages (Brendan, 4 October 2026)

Filed for the Alberta build, which waits for Brendan's word. Not yet transcribed, except Table 6 (Calgary-Field) and the Crowsnest part of
Table 151, which are already in the map. Brendan's answers for the build are in scans/og1948_cn/README.md.

Which tables are in Alberta (he could not tell from the pages alone):
- All in Alberta: 138 Calgary-Edmonton; 143 Calgary-Wimborne-Kneehill; 149 Calgary-Macleod-Lethbridge; 145 Lethbridge-Turin;
  141 South Edmonton-Leduc-Breton-Lacombe; 142 Red Deer-Rocky Mountain House; 137 Medicine Hat-Lomond-Aldersyde; 136 Bassano-Rosemary-Kneehill;
  151 Medicine Hat-Lethbridge (east of what is on the map).
- Alberta to a boundary: 150 Lethbridge-Cardston and Lethbridge-Coutts (on to Great Falls over the Great Northern); 144 Lloydminster-Willingdon-
  Edmonton (Lloydminster is on the boundary).
- Cross from Saskatchewan: 92 Wilkie-Hardisty-Camrose-Edmonton (Alberta from Hayter); 139 Kerrobert-Coronation-Lacombe (from Compeer);
  132 Swift Current-Empress-Bassano-Irricana (from Empress); 120 Moose Jaw-Shaunavon-Lethbridge (from Jaydot, by Manyberries and Foremost);
  133 Fox Valley-Schuler-Empress (weaves across: Schuler, Hilda and Empress are in Alberta).
- All in Saskatchewan, so out: 134 Hatton-Golden Prairie; 135 Consul-Notukeu-Valmarie; 140 Wickett-Verlo.

Larger captures (the files ending _LARGE) arrived the same day for Tables 92 (Wilkie-Edmonton part), 120, 138, 143 and 149/150: use those.
They settle the boundary stations: Table 92 has Macklin, Sask. then Hayter, Alta.; Table 120 has Altawan, Sask. then Jaydot, Alta.
"Mountain Time" on a table does not mean Alberta: the railways kept Mountain time across most of Saskatchewan too (Tables 134, 135 and 140,
all in Saskatchewan, are headed Mountain Time; the CP main line changes at Broadview and the CN at Watrous and Humboldt). Go by the "Alta." and
"Sask." printed after the boundary stations, and by the places themselves.
