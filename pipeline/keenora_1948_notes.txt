S.S. KEENORA, Selkirk Navigation Co. Ltd., Lake Winnipeg (Brendan, 20 Oct 2026; advertisement, scans/boats/keenora_ad.png, year not printed; Brendan: 1937. The Keenora ran until about 1960, so the pattern is taken as still good for 1948).
"6 Day Cruises on Lake Winnipeg ... weekly excursions during July and August." Leaves Redwood Dock, Winnipeg, every Monday at 12 noon for
Warren's Landing (north end of Lake Winnipeg), connecting with a boat for Norway House. Return fare $30 with berth, meals and the Norway House boat;
other sailings $24 (so some sailings did not include Norway House). "Also excursions to Lockport every Sunday." 100 passengers; only steel
passenger steamer on the lake. Phones Winnipeg and Selkirk.
Implied: down the Red River from Winnipeg past Lockport and Selkirk to the lake; 6 days out and back, so back at Winnipeg about Saturday.
