  Mon, Thu, Sat   Carcross 2.00 p.m. -> Golden Gate (passing) -> Taku Landing (for the Taku Tram to Atlin) -> back to Carcross 11.00 a.m. next day
  Wed, Fri, Sun   Carcross 2.00 p.m. -> Golden Gate (passing) -> West Taku Arm -> Ben-My-Chree (evening, dance on the freight deck) -> Carcross 11.00 a.m. next day

The week loops exactly: every trip ends at 11.00 a.m. the next morning, and the Monday trip returns on the Tuesday.
